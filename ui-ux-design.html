Frequently asked questions

Common Questions Before Starting a UI/UX
Design Project

Practical answers about our UX research, prototyping, design systems, and engineering handoff process.

Q. What UI/UX design services does Karmick Solutions provide?
We provide end-to-end UI/UX design including user research, wireframing, interactive prototyping, brand and logo design, marketing website design, SaaS web app UI, enterprise dashboards, and iOS/Android mobile app design.
Q. How do you ensure the design improves conversions and usability?
Every engagement begins with user journey mapping, stakeholder goals, and heuristic evaluation so layouts prioritize clear CTAs, reduced cognitive load, and measurable conversion outcomes.
Q. Do you create reusable design systems for engineering teams?
Yes. We deliver structured component libraries, typography scales, color tokens, and state specifications that make frontend development faster and visually consistent across releases.
Q. Can you redesign an existing legacy application or website?
Absolutely. We audit your current product to identify drop-off points and usability bottlenecks, then modernize the information architecture and visual UI without disrupting core business workflows.
Q. How long does a typical UI/UX design project take?
A focused website or MVP mobile app design typically takes 2 to 4 weeks, while full enterprise SaaS platforms with comprehensive design systems take 6 to 10 weeks.
Q. Can Karmick also handle frontend and full-stack development after design?
Yes. Our UI/UX designers work directly alongside our React, Next.js, Angular, Flutter, and backend engineers so your approved designs move smoothly into production.
Q. Will I be able to review interactive prototypes before development?
Yes, we share clickable prototypes at key milestones so your team can test flows, leave feedback, and validate user journeys prior to engineering.
